      This assessment is a pilot project run by UnityWerk. Questions or requests about your data: unitywerk@gmail.com.

      What we collect and why

      Your assessment answers (background, career break, experience, goals, skills, and confidence), plus your name and email address. We use this data for one purpose only: to create your personal career roadmap and send it to you by email. The legal basis is your consent, given by ticking the box before submitting.

      Where your data is stored

      Submissions are delivered to us through Formspree Inc. (USA), which participates in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and arrive in our email inbox. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and we won't send you anything beyond your roadmap unless you separately ask us to.

      How long we keep it

      Your answers are deleted within 30 days after your roadmap has been sent.

      Your rights

      You can ask at any time to see, correct, or delete the data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent — just email us. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
